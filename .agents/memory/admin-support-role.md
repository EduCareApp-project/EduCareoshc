---
name: Admin Support role
description: Defines the intentional access and visibility boundary for Admin Support staff accounts.
---

Admin Support has administrative permissions but is not part of the operational educator workforce. It must have no Home Site or Associated Sites and must not appear in educator-visible staff lists, compliance views or reports, guardian educator views, or school portal staff lists.

**Why:** This role supports administration across the organisation without being represented as a Site-based educator or compliance participant.

**How to apply:** Whenever adding a staff directory, Site assignment, school/guardian staff response, qualification compliance calculation, report, or exact Admin authorization check, treat Admin Support as Admin for access and exclude it from operational workforce data.
