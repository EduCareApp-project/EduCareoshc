---
name: Active-child learning lists and activity gaps
description: Active-only profile scope across learning lists and the meaning of recent or prolonged gaps.
---

The Educator dashboard's “Children with no active data” tile is about active children only. A child has recent activity if an observation, jotting, post, or planning cycle has been recorded for them within the rolling last 30 days. Show children with no recent activity; mark their badge red after three calendar months without recorded activity.

**Why:** The previous observation-only count did not reflect the other ways educators document a child's learning, and could include inactive or archived children. A newly enrolled child cannot have had a three-month gap before enrolment.

**How to apply:** Attribute posts and planning cycles only to children explicitly linked to them. Measure recent activity from the record's creation date; use a child's enrolment date as the starting point if they have no records. Keep inactive and archived children out of this tile.

Observation, jotting and planning-cycle child lists must also exclude inactive and archived profiles. Duplicate names are not a reason to merge profiles: status must be checked against the individual child ID. Hide records linked only to inactive children in the active learning lists, but preserve their historical records and existing planning associations.

**Why:** The user reported two entries with the same name in Jottings even though one profile was inactive, and repeated the active-only requirement across all four learning surfaces.

**How to apply:** Apply active status consistently to child selectors and active record feeds, while leaving child management and historical storage intact. Refresh those lists and the activity-gap tile when a profile's status changes.

For Posts, keep older organisation-wide/unlinked posts visible and preserve their original audience and historical child links while narrowing the staff learning feed. Do not reinterpret or change those older posts' audience without confirming the intended visibility with the user.

**Why:** Active-only browsing does not imply that earlier organisation-wide posts should lose their audience; the requested work explicitly required confirmation before changing it.

**How to apply:** Keep feed filtering separate from stored post associations and guardian-facing visibility. Ask before changing the audience of older organisation-wide posts.
