---
name: Child activity gaps
description: Meaning of recent and prolonged gaps in educator learning records.
---

The Educator dashboard's “Children with no active data” tile is about active children only. A child has recent activity if an observation, jotting, post, or planning cycle has been recorded for them within the rolling last 30 days. Show children with no recent activity; mark their badge red after three calendar months without recorded activity.

**Why:** The previous observation-only count did not reflect the other ways educators document a child's learning, and could include inactive or archived children. A newly enrolled child cannot have had a three-month gap before enrolment.

**How to apply:** Attribute posts and planning cycles only to children explicitly linked to them. Measure recent activity from the record's creation date; use a child's enrolment date as the starting point if they have no records. Keep inactive and archived children out of this tile.
