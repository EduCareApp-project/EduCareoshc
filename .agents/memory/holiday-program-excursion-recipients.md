---
name: Holiday-program excursion recipients
description: Cross-site child eligibility for excursion permission forms.
---

Guardian recipient verification for excursions must accept active children from every site, not only children assigned to the excursion’s selected site.

**Why:** Holiday programs combine children from multiple services, so normal-site assignment does not determine excursion attendance.

**How to apply:** Keep staff access and excursion ownership scoped to the selected site, but resolve each verified active guardian’s linked active children across all sites when creating recipient assignments.

Staff may hide an individual recipient’s excursion from the guardian app without deleting the recipient or their response history.

**Why:** A child may no longer attend, while the service still needs an accurate audit trail of who received and responded to the permission form.

**How to apply:** The removal action hides the guardian assignment, deletes its in-app notification, and excludes it from later reminders. Guardian “Upcoming Excursions” includes the excursion date itself, then hides it from the next Melbourne calendar day.
