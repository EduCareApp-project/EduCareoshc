---
name: Learning compliance planning scope
description: User-defined historical and optional future-plan coverage in the Planning outcome tile
---

On Learning Compliance, the Planning tile should reflect the selected past period and, when the educator ticks “Include future plans”, all future open plans for the selected site.

**Why:** The user explicitly wants educators to opt into future planning coverage without limiting it to the length of the historical reporting period.

**How to apply:** Future inclusion has no lookahead cap. Exclude closed future plans and templates; historical completed plans remain evidence within the selected past period. Keep this control scoped to Planning rather than changing Observations & Posts.
