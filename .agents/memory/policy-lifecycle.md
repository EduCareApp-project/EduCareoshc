name: Policy lifecycle and distributions
description: Saved policy recipients and staff-specific inactive acknowledgement history
---

Policy distributions are saved lists of individual staff, not live role-based audiences. New staff must be added individually to existing policies.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose “Saved staff list” so managers can add new hires to the policies they still need to acknowledge.

**How to apply:** Role selection determines current recipients when distributing; do not automatically enroll future hires, or rebuild saved lists on application startup.

When viewing as a staff member, show every active policy even when that person is not in the distribution list. Offer managers an add-to-distribution action for missing recipients. Put only inactive policies acknowledged by that person in a collapsed section at the bottom.

**Why:** The user wants a complete active-policy onboarding checklist plus historical signed-policy access without clutter.

**How to apply:** Historical acknowledgement of any version qualifies an inactive policy for that staff member’s history. Deactivation must retain acknowledgements and recipients; it is not deletion or a version update.
